Gold Rate Pure Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Gold Rate Pure ("the app", "we") shows estimated gold and silver prices for India and offers simple tools such as a jewellery bill checker. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes and the choices you have.

Information that stays on your phone

Your name, gender and profile photo (all optional), settings, theme, background photo, bill calculations, goals, price alerts and the list of gold you own are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not run any server that receives this information, and we do not sell it.

  • Camera and photos: if you scan a bill or add a photo, the image is read on your device (text recognition runs on the phone) and is never uploaded by us.
  • Fingerprint / screen lock: if you lock your vault, Android checks your fingerprint or screen lock. The app never sees or stores your fingerprint.
  • Notifications: price alerts and the morning reminder are checked and scheduled on your phone. To check alerts, the app downloads public prices in the background; no personal information is sent.
  • Android backup: if Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup, under Google's backup terms. You control this in your phone's settings.

Prices

The app downloads public gold and silver prices from our price feed (hosted on GitHub Pages) and from gold-api.com. These requests do not contain your name, account or any data you have entered. Like any internet request, the servers that answer them can see your device's IP address; we do not receive or store it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and keep the service secure, Google may collect and share the following, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the advertising ID
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Where required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything you enter stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time with "Delete all my data" in the app, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted under Google's own policies; you can manage it at myadcenter.google.com.

Children

The app is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Accuracy

Prices are estimates based on international spot prices, Indian import duty and a market premium. They are for information only and are not financial advice. Please confirm the final price with your jeweller before buying or selling.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
