Gold Rate Pure Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Gold Rate Pure ("the app") shows estimated gold and silver prices for India. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information that stays on your phone

Your settings, theme, background photo, bill calculations and the list of gold you own are stored only on your device. We do not receive, store or sell them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Prices

The app downloads public price data. These requests do not include any personal information.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and app usage information to show and measure ads, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Google settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone. No information leaves the device for this.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Accuracy

Prices are estimates based on international spot prices and Indian import duty. They are for information only and are not financial advice. Confirm prices with your jeweller before buying or selling.
