Gold Rate Pure: how to delete your data

Gold Rate Pure has no accounts and no server that stores your personal information. Everything you enter is kept only on your own phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time, instantly.

Delete everything from inside the app

  1. Open Gold Rate Pure.
  2. Tap Me (bottom right).
  3. Open Backup, export & import.
  4. Tap Delete all my data, then confirm.

Or simply uninstall the app, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Gold Rate Pure → Storage → Clear storage.

What is deleted

All of it, immediately and permanently: your name, gender and profile photo, settings, gold vault items and their photos, goals, price alerts, occasions, reminders, saved calculations and cached prices.

What is kept

We keep nothing, because we never receive this data. There is no retention period on our side.

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Data that Google collects for ads (such as the advertising ID, approximate location, app interactions, crash logs and diagnostics) is held by Google, not by us, and is kept and deleted under Google's policies. You can:

  • delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads);
  • manage or delete ad activity at myadcenter.google.com.
